# Architecture decision records

ADRs explain important project decisions in simple language. An ADR records a decision; it does not prove that the design works or grant human acceptance to implementation.

Create new records as `ADR-<number>-<slug>.md`, keep one decision per file, and link the relevant experiments, evidence, human decisions, reversals, and affected implementation records. This template intentionally contains no project decisions.

## Status meanings

- `Proposed`: recommended, but the accountable owner has not accepted it.
- `Experimenting`: the direction is being tested and evidence is incomplete.
- `Accepted`: approved by the accountable owner with its required evidence.
- `Deferred`, `Rejected`, or `Superseded`: not currently active.

Do not use an ADR to approve legal purpose, privacy policy, retention, access, budget, production risk, or deployment without the accountable human decision.
