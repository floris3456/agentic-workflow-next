# Research review checklist

- Inputs exactly match the manifest and prompt allowlist.
- Facts, assumptions, inferences, estimates, recommendations, unknowns, human decisions, and CLI experiments are separated.
- Current claims use dated primary sources.
- Technology choices include alternatives, rejection reasons, maintenance, licensing, security, and operational fit.
- Interfaces, schemas, state machines, failure recovery, rollout, rollback, and tests are implementation-ready.
- Numerical claims remain provisional until measured.
- Conflicts with accepted reviews are explicit.
- Residual risks and confidence are stated without “bulletproof” claims.
- The result leads to ADR proposals, a human decision, or a bounded experiment.
